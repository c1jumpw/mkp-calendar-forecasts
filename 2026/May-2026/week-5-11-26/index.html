Event Summaries by Region

Downtown Atlanta & Midtown

🔴 Red Hat Summit: Connect Atlanta — May 11–14
Atlanta's flagship tech enterprise event of the week. Running four days at the Georgia World Congress Center, it targets IT leaders, software developers, CISOs, and open-source contributors through a dense schedule of keynotes, lab sessions, and ecosystem partner days. Tracks cover hybrid cloud, Kubernetes, AI/ML workload management, Linux, and supply chain security. The Expo Networking Reception offers structured peer connections, and the "Hallway Track" is legendary for unplanned high-value conversations. Ticket range from $799 (group, 3+) to $1,999 at the door.
🎓 Clear Admit MBA Fair – May 11 · 5 PM
A focused admissions fair where top MBA programs meet prospective applicants face-to-face at Georgia Tech's Scheller College of Business. Perfect for mid-career professionals ready to explore graduate business education. Free to attend; bring business cards and questions for admissions officers.
🚶 YEC Beltline Networking Night — Every Monday · 6:30 PM
A recurring casual outdoor networking event on the Atlanta Beltline's Eastside Trail, hosted by the Young Entrepreneurs Collaborative. No agenda, no badges — just founders, creatives, and professionals walking and talking. One of Atlanta's most organic networking formats and completely free.
💬 ABC Tuesday Talks Virtual Roundtable — May 12 · 8:30 AM
The Atlanta Black Chambers' weekly virtual roundtable hosted on Zoom by Executive Director Melvin Coleman. Covers timely updates for Black entrepreneurs, features guest speakers, and spotlights chamber members. Free and accessible from anywhere.
🌐 ABC GOC Weekly Power Hour — May 13 · 8:30 AM
The Global Operations Committee's focused online Power Hour where participants gain knowledge from international business owners. A short, high-signal virtual session for the globally-minded Atlanta entrepreneur. Free to attend via the ABC calendar.
🍹 ABC Black Health Social Mixer — May 13 · 6 PM
Hosted by the Atlanta Black Chambers Health & Wellness Committee, this after-work mixer blends community building with business networking around health themes. Free, in-person, and welcoming — confirm venue closer to the date via the ABC calendar.
🤝 Atlanta Business Social (Strive Networking) — May 13 · 6 PM
A well-reviewed Wednesday evening professional networking meetup organized by Strive Networking / Atlanta Business Social. Draws roughly 20 cross-industry professionals in an informal format. Great for consultants, early-stage entrepreneurs, and career-focused professionals seeking genuine peer connections. Free, venue via Meetup RSVP.
💼 Atlanta Career Fair — May 14
A multi-employer in-person hiring event connecting job seekers with top Atlanta-area companies across industries. Free to attend; business-professional attire recommended. Bring printed resumes and be ready for on-the-spot recruiter conversations. Venue to be confirmed via Eventbrite.
🏥 SGNA 53rd Annual Course — May 17 · 8 AM · Hyatt Regency Atlanta
The Society of Gastroenterology Nurses and Associates' premier national conference for continuing education and certification. Held at the Hyatt Regency Atlanta (265 Peachtree St NE), it covers clinical skill-building, certification pathways, and professional development for GI nursing and endoscopy professionals. CME/CE credits available for eligible attendees.

Features free educational seminars on franchise law, financing models, and business operations. Ideal for anyone seriously exploring entrepreneurship through the franchise route.
